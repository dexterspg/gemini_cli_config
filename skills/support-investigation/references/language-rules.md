# Support Investigation: Customer Language Rules

## Confidence Tiers

Align language to confirmed evidence. Never overstate confidence.

| Confidence | Meaning | Language Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Single hypothesis, confirmed by direct evidence. | "The logs confirm that..." / "The failure is caused by..." |
| **Probable** | Strong match, 1-2 alternatives, needs confirmation. | "Our investigation points to..." / "The logs suggest..." |
| **Suspected** | Plausible, multiple competing causes, missing evidence. | "We believe this may be related to..." / "A possible cause is..." |

## Hedging Principles

- **Commit to timing:** "Update by EOD" is better than "Resolved soon".
- **Separate Fact from Hypothesis:** State confirmed facts first, then theories.
- **Avoid Pressure-Induced Overpromising:** Under-confidence is safer for trust than over-confidence.

## Rules
- NEVER say "identified root cause" unless it is **Confirmed**.
- NEVER say "caused by X" unless it is **Confirmed**.
- ALWAYS match the Escalation Package confidence label to the language used here.
